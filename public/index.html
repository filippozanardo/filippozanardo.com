      02 / About

      Making things.
      Making sense of them.

      I’ve been building software professionally since 2000, across web development and digital products. I also spent years working as a systems administrator, an experience that still shapes how I think about the whole system.

      Over the years I’ve worked as a developer, entrepreneur, freelancer, technical lead and CTO — building everything from WordPress websites and custom Laravel applications to business platforms, SaaS products and interactive experiences.

      I’m comfortable moving between the bigger picture and the code: understanding the problem, making technical decisions, working with designers and teams, and then getting my hands dirty building the thing.

      Today I spend most of my time building with PHP, Laravel and WordPress, while staying deliberately close to both architecture and code. I also use AI as part of my everyday development process to explore, build and iterate faster — without outsourcing the responsibility of understanding what I ship.

      I’m also a dad. It keeps me curious, grounded and occasionally very tired.
