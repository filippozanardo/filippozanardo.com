02 / About

Making things.
Making sense of them.

I started working professionally in 2000, moving between development, systems and infrastructure. Since then, I’ve been an entrepreneur, a freelancer, a technical lead and a CTO.

The thread through all of it is simple: I like building things, and understanding how they should work. Sometimes that means looking at the whole system. Sometimes it means getting one small detail right.

I use AI every day in my development process: to explore ideas, build and iterate. It’s part of how I work, alongside experience, curiosity and the responsibility to understand what I ship.

I’m also a dad. Work is a big part of my life, but it’s one part of it.

I still like being close to the code.
