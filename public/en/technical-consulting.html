Technical consulting

Technical consulting for better software decisions.

I help you understand how an application is working, decide what to address first and plan the next stage of development.

How I can help

  • Technical audits and code review

    I review the code structure, dependencies and key workflows. I document the issues I find, their impact and an order for tackling them.

  • Architecture and technology choices

    I compare options against product needs, operational constraints and future maintenance. Together we define a practical way forward.

  • Technical support for teams and agencies

    I join reviews, architecture discussions or working sessions on specific problems and share my reasoning with the people who will maintain the project.

Consulting can be a standalone engagement with an assessment and action plan. Your team can implement the changes, or I can join the development work.
